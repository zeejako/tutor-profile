06 — Integration of Technology • WSU

Technology that makes
IS2 click, not confuse.

I don't use technology to show off. I use it to make hard ideas simple — for every student, even with low data.

🎥 Visuals & Board

Draw.io, Canva, Whiteboard
I draw ERDs live with colours. Students screenshot and keep them. Visual memory > reading slides.

💬 WhatsApp & Teams

Low-data support
After tutorial, I send voice notes, short videos, and my practice test PDF on WhatsApp group. No student left behind.

🗄️ SQL Practice

Live SQL + SQLite
I run queries live — students see results instantly. SELECT becomes exciting when you see your library data appear.

📚 WSU Moodle / WiSeUp

All notes, past papers, and my lesson plans organised by week, so students can revise anytime.

🚀 Skill Link App

My Advanced Diploma project — an app where IS2 students find notes, past papers & library resources in one place. Technology built FOR WSU students, BY a WSU student.
