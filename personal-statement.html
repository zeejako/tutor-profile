02 — Personal Statement

My love for teaching
was born from struggle.

My name is Zizile Jako. I am an Advanced Diploma in ICT student in Application Development, and a tutor for second-year students in Information Systems 2.

I did not start as the top student. I started as the quiet girl at the back — copying notes I didn't understand, too shy to ask, "What does this mean?"

Then one tutor changed everything. She didn't shout. She didn't make me feel small. She used a simple spaza shop example to explain a database. Suddenly — it clicked. And in that click, I found my purpose.

That is why my personal statement is simple: I teach with love. Every tutorial is a safe space. No question is stupid. Every small win — a query that finally works, an ERD that finally makes sense — we celebrate like a graduation.

"If they don't understand, I explain differently — not louder."

Every student deserves a tutor who believes in them before they believe in themselves.

That is my philosophy. IS is about people solving human problems — not just computers.

🎯 My Mission: Make IS2 possible for everyone.
❤️ My Values: Empathy, clarity, consistency, patience.
🚀 My Dream: My students becoming tutors themselves one day.
Why I Tutor IS2?
Because I know the fear. And I know the joy when fear becomes confidence.
