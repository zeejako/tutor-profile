04 — Training & Development • WSU

I did not just become
a tutor. I was trained
to be one.

Walter Sisulu University believes great tutors are made, not born. Here is how WSU trained me — and how I keep growing.

🎓 WSU Tutor Training I Completed

✅ Initial Tutor Training — WSU Centre for Learning & Teaching
Roles, ethics, boundaries, how to facilitate not lecture. How to handle shy students, late students, stressed students.
✅ Facilitation Skills Workshop
Active learning, questioning techniques, group work. I learned: "Don't answer — ask a better question."
✅ Assessment & Feedback Training
How to give feedback that builds, not breaks. How to mark fairly.
✅ POPIA & Professionalism
Protecting student data, attendance registers, confidentiality.
✅ Ongoing Mentorship
Monthly meetings with lecturer . We reflect, we improve.

📈 My Growth as an ICT Student

Advanced Diploma in ICT — Application Development
What it gives my tutoring:
• I don't just teach theory, I build systems live
• I show students code working, not just slides
• My final project — Skill Link — started as an IS2 example!

✨ What training changed for me:
Before: I talked too much.
After: I let students discover.
Before: I feared hard questions.
After: I say "Let's solve it together."
