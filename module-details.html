Information Systems 2:
Not just computers.
People, processes & problems.

IS2 is where students learn how businesses really think. How data flows. How systems are born. My job is to make it feel alive — not scary.

IS2 • Second Year
Walter Sisulu University • Faculty of Engineering, Built Environment and Technology
Students: 20+  |  Mode: Contact + Online  |  My Role: Tutor

🗄️ 1. Databases & SQL

Tables, queries, relationships. I teach with spaza shop example: "Customer buys bread" = INSERT. We practice until SQL feels like language, not code.

My Example: Library System = Members + Books + Loans

🔷 2. ERD & Modelling

Entity Relationship Diagrams. Students fear crow's foot. I draw it on board with colors — One library has Many books. Suddenly it makes sense.

Tool: Draw.io + Whiteboard + Real-life stories

🔄 3. SDLC & Systems

System Development Life Cycle. Planning, Analysis, Design, Implementation. I turn it into building a real app — like my Skill Link app idea.

Activity: Design a system for WSU Library

💻 4. Business Processes

How does a business actually work? We map processes — from ordering stock to reporting. Students learn to think like analysts.

🧩 5. Problem Solving

Case studies, test prep, past papers. I give them my practice test so they walk into test with confidence, not panic.

✨ My WSU Touch

At WSU, our students are resilient. My teaching respects that. I use local examples — township businesses, WSU admin systems — so IS2 feels like home.
