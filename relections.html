07 — Reflections • Growing Every Week

What tutoring
taught me about me.

💪 Strengths I Discovered

• I explain with stories, not jargon
• I notice quiet students and invite them in
• I am consistent — students trust I will always show up prepared
• My Advanced Diploma coding helps me demo systems live

🌱 Challenges I Faced

• At first I talked too much — I learned to ask, not tell
• Some students came late — I learned to start with a recap so no one feels lost
• Big groups (40+) — I learned to use group work and peer teaching

📖 My Reflection Journal

Week 2: "I was nervous. I prepared 20 slides. Students looked bored. I scrapped slides and drew on board — they woke up!"

Week 5: "I tried my spaza shop ERD example. A student said 'Ohhh! So foreign key is like a customer ID!' — I knew my method works."

Week 8: "Now students ask ME for practice tests before tests. They feel confident. I feel like a real teacher."

Tutoring made me a better student too.

🔁 What I Will Do Better

1. More one-on-one check-ins for shy students
2. revision on WhatsApp
3. More real WSU examples — library, admin, student systems
4. Keep learning — attend more WSU workshops
